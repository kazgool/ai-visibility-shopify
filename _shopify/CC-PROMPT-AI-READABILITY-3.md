Third batch, same deploy, nothing pushed. Same hard rules as the two earlier prompts in _shopify/. Read _shopify/audit-logs-2026-09-11/engine-after.md first: it is the evidence for this batch.

Why: the generic question template ("What {label} does X have?" / "Ce {label} are X?") restates the facts list as questions, so it adds no information, publishes every extraction error a second time (visibly and as FAQPage), and the cap of six cuts safety labels (allergens, warnings) while trivia wins. It goes. What replaces it is a new FAQ engine that must work on any store, any vertical, any language the app supports (en, ro), including stores whose descriptions have no structure at all. No part of it may be tuned to one merchant. Republica BIO is one test store among many, never the design target.

Acceptance bar, set by Marius, non-negotiable: every question and answer the new engine produces, on every product of every test store, is judged; the error rate must be at most 1 percent, measured on stores the engine was NOT tuned on. Above 1 percent the batch is not done: fix the class of error, never the instance, and re-measure. Do not wire the engine into any live path until the bar is met.

1. Corpus, before any code. Build _shopify/corpus/ (gitignored; commit only the manifest and the scripts):
   - the two catalogues already used by scripts/audit-engine-run.ts;
   - public products.json from at least 12 further Shopify stores across at least 6 verticals (food or supplements, cosmetics, fashion, furniture or home, electronics or tools, pets or toys), at least 4 of them in Romanian and at least 6 in English. Start from the stores listed in _shopify/audit-logs-2026-09-10/C-jsonld-prevalence.md; add others found by search. Only the public /products.json endpoint, paginated, read only, polite (one request per second, stop at 250 products per store), honour robots.txt.
   - Split the stores into a dev set (tune on it) and a hold-out set (at least 5 stores, at least 2 Romanian, at least 2 English, never opened while writing rules). Write the split into the manifest before writing any rule.
   Report per store: vertical, language, product count, share of descriptions with any heading or bold paragraph start, share with a line ending in "?".

2. Engine, pure, app/engine/faq.ts, no Shopify, no Prisma, no I/O. Input: product (title, descriptionHtml, options with values, vendor, product type), facts, business record, content language, preset id, shop-level mappings (item 3). Output: an ordered list of { q, a, source, sourceSpan } where sourceSpan points into the description when the answer comes from it. Sources, each independent:
   a. Merchant questions: a heading, bold paragraph start or line ending in "?" followed by text. The question is the merchant's text as written (cleaned), the answer is the text that follows up to the next heading, cut at a sentence boundary at 600 characters.
   b. Description sections by intent: headings (h2 to h6, strong or b at a paragraph start, a line ending in ":") classified into a small set of intents: usage, composition or materials, care or storage, warnings or safety, dimensions or size, benefits or features, contents of the package, compatibility, suitability (who it is for). Keyword lists per intent per language are derived from the dev-set corpus (frequency of heading texts, with the counts in the report), not written from memory. Question text per intent per language comes from the phrase table (phrases.ts). A heading that matches no intent produces nothing.
   c. Shop mappings (item 3).
   d. Preset templates: each existing trade preset may carry question templates for its own group labels; only presets whose groups actually have values ask. Ship templates only where the dev corpus shows the group produces clean values.
   e. Shopify data: variants ("Which options is X available in?" / RO equivalent) only when an option has more than one value and the option is not the default "Title"; vendor only when it differs from the shop name.
   f. Business: delivery, returns, warranty, payment (existing phrasing).
   Rules: no answer, no question; no duplicate questions (normalise and compare); the same answer text never appears under two questions; order is warnings or safety, usage, composition, care, dimensions, contents, compatibility, suitability, benefits, merchant questions, preset, variants, business; cap from a setting, default 8, safety never cut by the cap (it counts toward it but is placed first). Remove the generic label template and its phrases; keep the existing label-specific templates only if the judge (item 5) finds them within the bar, otherwise they go too.

3. Shop mappings in the Dictionary screen: "Section heading -> question" pairs and "Group -> question" templates, both optional, per shop, stored with the dictionary, validated (question must contain the title placeholder, plain characters). Plus, per dictionary group, "Show on the product page" (default on) that the visible facts list honours; facts stay in metafields and the mirror regardless.

4. Wire-up is item 6, not now.

5. The judge, on every Q&A of every product of every store. Write _shopify/corpus/judge-rubric.md first: a Q&A is an ERROR when any of these holds: (1) the answer does not answer the question; (2) the answer states something not present in the product's own data (description, facts, options, business record); (3) the answer is cut so that its meaning changes or is lost; (4) the question is ungrammatical or unreadable in its language; (5) the question is about something else than this product (store-wide text in a product question is allowed only for business questions); (6) a safety or warning section exists in the description and no safety question was produced; (7) duplicate question or duplicate answer on the same product. Judge in batches with subagents, each verdict logged as JSON (store, product, q, a, verdict, rule, reason). Report per store and per source: produced, errors, error rate, with denominators, and every error listed. Dev set and hold-out reported separately; the bar applies to the hold-out total and to every hold-out store with at least 50 Q&A. Iterate on dev, re-run on hold-out; if you open hold-out products to fix a rule, that store moves to dev and you add a new hold-out store.

6. Wire-up, only after the bar holds: replace the question builder in the live path with faq.ts (every caller grepped and counted), FAQPage in the body snippet built from the same list, mirror and llms.txt use the same list. Re-run audit-engine-run.ts on the two local catalogues: before and after, questions per product, per source, every removed question listed.

7. Visible facts, measured not fixed: run the same judge on the facts list as it would show on the page (label: value), same rubric rules 2 and 3, on the same stores. Report the error rate per store and per group. This decides which groups the merchant should switch off and whether facts show by default; do not change defaults in this batch, report only.

Docs and handover as before, plus: the corpus manifest and split, the intent keyword lists with their corpus counts, the phrase table additions in en and ro for approval, the judge results table (dev, hold-out, per store, per source), the facts error table, and a plain statement: bar met or not met, with the number.
