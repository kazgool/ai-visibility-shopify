# Full audit: functionality, UI, UX, and what would make it sell

2 September 2026. Covers the published app as of commit `6a71a6c` plus the
uncommitted CI fix from this morning.

## How this was produced, and what it is not

Two independent readers went through every route, service, worker task,
webhook, the storefront block and the mirror renderer, one looking for what
the code does and fails to do, the other walking it as four merchants would.
Neither saw the other's output. I then opened the live app on
`mrdigital-dev` in a browser and looked at the dashboard, Products,
Diagnostics, SEO and Plan screens as rendered, and spot-checked the top
findings of both readers against the source before accepting them. Every
finding below carries a file and line; anything I could not point to was
dropped.

Not done: no test was executed, no long job was timed, the product editor
and Collections were not opened in the browser (the embedded iframe froze
twice on click), no accessibility pass beyond colour, and the engine,
`theme-scan.server.ts` and `catalogue.server.ts` were sampled rather than
read in full. Second-render claims that were read from code and not pressed
are marked as such.

## The verdict in ten lines

The engine, the gates on the SEO paths and the honesty rules hold up. The
product is not lying anywhere. What it does badly is the first sixty
seconds and the last ten: a new merchant sees eleven buttons and eight red
tiles with no ordered path, and a merchant who did the work sees numbers
that do not move to reward them. The single decision that matters before
Republica BIO is the SEO before-snapshot, which the spec calls
unrecoverable and which does not exist. Two write paths have no
entitlement gate at all. A failed pass renders as a real measurement of
zero. Four screens use words a shop owner does not know in the one
sentence that is meant to earn trust. And the most convincing thing the
app can show, that a crawler read a page which did not exist before
install, is computed nowhere despite the data being in the table.

## 1. Blocking: fix before the next merchant is onboarded

### 1.1 The SEO before-snapshot does not exist

`app/routes/app.plans.tsx:115` grants the unlock with
`grantSeoUnlock(shop.id, ...)` and nothing else. `prisma/schema.prisma`
has no snapshot model. SEO-WORKSPACE-PRD section 9d says there is no second
chance at a first state, and that is now literally true: after the first
`seo_apply` the pre-engagement meta titles, descriptions and JSON-LD node
counts are gone. Republica BIO is the first shop where the before/after
argument is the deliverable.

Fix: a one-time, never-overwritten snapshot row written inside
`grantSeoUnlock` before any SEO write is possible: date, meta title count,
meta description count, node types, conflicts. One migration, one write.

### 1.2 Two write paths have no entitlement check

`app/routes/app.business.tsx:42` and `app/routes/app.dictionary.tsx:57`.
Both actions authenticate, find the shop and write. Neither calls
`hasPaidAccess`. The parent route gate in `app.tsx` does not run before a
child action in Remix, so a POST from a shop with no subscription and no
comp writes shop metafields (`app.business.tsx:72`) or overwrites the
dictionary Setting. This is the same class as the free-tier bypass fixed on
1 September; these two were missed then.

Fix: the same two-line check `app.collections.tsx:104` already has.

### 1.3 `bulk_extract` and `bulk_alt_text` re-check nothing at execution

`worker/tasks.ts:48` and `:408` go straight to the write. Every other
write task (`bulk_collections:623`, `seo_watch:537`, `seo_queue_build:751`,
`seo_apply` via `seo-bulk.server.ts:122`) re-checks
`mayProcessAutomatically` and sets `refused`. A job queued while paid and
executed after cancellation, or enqueued by any future path, writes the
whole catalogue unchecked.

Fix: the same check and the same `refused` status.

### 1.4 A failed pass renders as a measurement of zero

`app/routes/app._index.tsx:69-72` picks `lastRun` with no status filter.
A failed run's report is `{ error }` (`worker/tasks.ts:87`), which is
truthy, so `:379` computes `covered = report.sampled - report.none` as
`NaN`, the Coverage tile shows `0%` with the hint `NaN produce attributes`,
and the summary line at `:674` reads `undefined products read`. A merchant
reads this as "the app found nothing", which is the opposite of what
happened.

Fix: only treat a report as figures when `status === "done"`; render the
failure by name otherwise. `seo-queue-metrics.ts` already does this for the
SEO tiles; copy the rule.

### 1.5 The first setup step sends a free shop to the pricing page

`app/routes/app._index.tsx:803` renders `Open dictionary` as step one of
Setup. `app/routes/app.tsx:34` lists the free routes and `/app/dictionary`
is not one of them, so the button lands on "Choose a plan" with no
explanation. The nav shows Dictionary, Collections and Business to a free
shop for the same reason. This is a documented decision in FREE-TIER-SPEC
section 5, and it is still the worst first impression the app can give:
the screen told them to press it.

Fix: either read-only dictionary for free shops, or grey the button and
the three nav items with one line saying what opens them. The gate itself
can stay.

### 1.6 Eleven live controls and no order on a fresh install

`app._index.tsx:516` Check now, `:643` Preview changes, `:650` Fill
catalogue, `:704` Write missing alt text, `:778` Open theme editor,
`:803-811` three more, plus Check again and See why. Two of them carry
`variant="primary"` in different cards. EXPERIENCE-PRD section 3 specifies
a four-step ladder with exactly one enabled primary action at a time; the
screen is a flat checklist (`:733-790`) with no locked state.

Seen live: on the dev store the first fold is four tiles, then eight red
crawler tiles reading "Sees the password page", then the amber banner. A
merchant with a password-protected pre-launch store gets the same wall and
nothing above it tells them it is expected.

Fix: build the ladder. One primary at a time, the rest secondary until
reached.

### 1.7 The app embed, without which nothing is published, is item five below the fold

`app._index.tsx:767`. A merchant can run a 400-product pass, see success
everywhere, and publish nothing to the storefront, because the only signal
is a caution icon fifth in a checklist in the right column.

Fix: when `embed.active` is false, a critical banner above the metric row
with the deep link. Also `embed-check.server.ts:77` sets `unreadable: true`
when the theme settings cannot be parsed, and no screen reads it, so an
unknown is currently rendered as "embed off". Render the unknown as
unknown.

### 1.8 "Fill catalogue" writes every product on one click

`app._index.tsx:650-658`. No modal, no count, and it sits next to
"Preview changes" at equal size with nothing on the card saying preview
writes nothing.

Fix: confirmation naming the real number, and demote preview to a
secondary link with "writes nothing" under it.

## 2. Needs fix

### 2.1 Success is invisible on the product editor, and revert shows the pre-revert value

`app/routes/app.products.$id.tsx` returns `{ saved }`, `{ altSaved }`,
`{ capsuleSaved }`, `{ capsuleReset }`, `{ reset }`, `{ seoReverted }`, and
the component renders only `actionData?.error` (`:679`). Nothing confirms
any of the six writes. Worse, `:631-632` seed the SEO fields with
`useState(seo?.title ?? "")` and never resync, so after "Revert to before
the app" the loader has the old value and the field still shows the new
one. Read from code, not pressed; it is the exact failure class named in
CLAUDE.md and the one I would press first.

Also: four Save buttons share one `busy` flag off `nav.state`, so pressing
any one spins all four, and they are separate forms, so editing attributes
and pressing the images Save discards the attribute edits silently.

Fix: per-form fetchers, a success banner per intent (the dictionary screen
already does it at `app.dictionary.tsx:113`), and `key` the SEO fields on
the loader value.

### 2.2 "Reset to automatic" is destructive with no confirmation

`app.products.$id.tsx:1225-1230`, `tone="critical"`, one click removes the
human-protected flag. The next pass may overwrite what they typed.

### 2.3 Dashboard headline figures are overwritten by a preview and never moved by the free tier

`app._index.tsx:69-72` feeds the tiles from whichever of `dry_run` or
`bulk_extract` ran last, so a preview after the real pass replaces the real
numbers. And the three free-tier writes go through `extractOneProduct`
(`app.products._index.tsx:263`), which produces no JobRun report, so a free
shop that processed three products returns to a dashboard still reading
"-" and "run a check to find out". The proof the product works is invisible
on the screen the merchant returns to.

Fix: metrics from the last completed write when one exists, labelled as a
preview otherwise; and a count of readable products from metafields that
does not depend on a job having run.

### 2.4 Crawler counts are shown as proof while unverified

`crawler-hits.server.ts:38` matches the raw user agent by substring.
CRAWLER-HITS-SPEC section 5 says the number may be shown only if verified,
or not shown. `app._index.tsx:616` shows it. The card's method line says
"real requests" but not "by user agent as sent, unverified". Bing's own
documentation, checked today, says user agents are trivially spoofed and
offers a verification tool; the WordPress plugin does forward-confirmed
reverse DNS since 1.7.7; Shopify does not.

Fix, short term: one clause in the method line. Long term: port the
verification.

### 2.5 The storefront password can only be entered on a screen most shops cannot open

`app.diagnostics.tsx:440` tells the merchant the password is entered on the
SEO screen. The only writer is `app.seo.tsx:178`, on a route that returns
"Not enabled for this shop" without the SEO unlock (`:91-93`). A paying,
password-protected pre-launch store is told where to go and cannot go
there, and every scan reports the password wall for ever.

Fix: move the field to Diagnostics. Also `app.seo.tsx:181-185` stores it in
plaintext in `Setting`; encrypt or document.

### 2.6 "metafields" carries the trust sentence on three screens

`app._index.tsx:437`, `app.products._index.tsx:454`, `app.plans.tsx:387`:
"What gets written stays written, in your own Shopify metafields, whether
you subscribe or not." This is the one sentence that has to land with a
doubting merchant and it hinges on a word they do not know.

Fix: "in your own product fields in Shopify".

### 2.7 The Products table has up to ten columns and blank thumbnails

`app.products._index.tsx:526-537`. Seen live at 1568px: fits, but every
thumbnail cell is an empty white square, the "Image text" column is "-" on
every row, and the "What AI reads" cell renders a full sentence when a
product is unpublished. At 1280px this will wrap or scroll inside the
iframe.

Fix: drop the thumbnail column or load the image; fold Attributes,
Questions and Summary into one Published cell; move the long sentence to a
tooltip.

### 2.8 Product filters filter the current 25 rows, and the dashboard deep-links into one

`app.products._index.tsx:208` filters after a `first: 25` query;
`app._index.tsx:806` links to `?filter=no_attributes`. On a big catalogue
the link opens an empty page saying "try another page".

### 2.9 The free crawler check is blocked by any running job

`app._index.tsx:201` takes a lock on any queued or running JobRun with no
`kind` filter; the free tier's headline feature cannot run while alt text
or a pass is in progress.

### 2.10 Diagnostics, seen live

Leads with a method paragraph and a button; the verdict card is second
(`app.diagnostics.tsx:250-265` then `:323-345`). The verdict rows show the
raw cause code `password_page` and US-format timestamps
`9/1/2026, 9:27:10 AM` to a Romanian merchant. The request log shows every
row as a green `other` badge, which reads as good news for traffic the app
explicitly does not recognise. The prewritten host message is a read-only
TextField with `onChange={() => {}}` and no copy button (`:548-555`).

Fix: verdict first as "N of 8 crawlers can read your store", cause codes
mapped to sentences, dates in the shop locale, `other` in neutral tone, a
copy button.

### 2.11 SEO screen, seen live

Tiles say "50 of 50" and the result banner says "already matched: 100" with
no unit; the 100 is fields, not products. The term-gap card lists "cm
colour", "people dimensions", "cm includes", "80", "110" and "includes
chairs" as terms the titles lack. Those are tokeniser noise from units and
numbers, and on a card whose whole claim is "counted from your own text"
they cost credibility with every merchant who reads past the first line.

Fix: unit on the banner; drop bare numbers and unit-led bigrams from the
term-gap list (`app/engine/term-gap.ts`).

### 2.12 Plan screen, seen live

A comped store sees both plan cards with a primary "Choose Standard" under a
banner saying no subscription is needed. The worked example, which
DESIGN-BRIEF section 9 calls the most persuasive element on the screen, is
third, below both price cards, and renders nothing when the newest product
yields no attributes (`app.plans.tsx:289`). The two entry codes are two
identical plain-text prompts with the same help text (`:407`, `:458`).

### 2.13 The Readability card cites an unattributed 1.4 million prompt study

`app.products.$id.tsx:794-798`. Name and link it, or drop the number.

### 2.14 The Business screen implies the FAQ is visible on the page

`app.business.tsx:259-266`: "As buyer questions on every product". The
block emits only `application/ld+json` (`ai-visibility.liquid:283-297`);
shoppers see nothing. A merchant will check and conclude it is broken.

### 2.15 IndexNow is always on, and the free tier pings it

`indexnow.server.ts:152` has `setEnabled` with no caller.
`extract.server.ts:404` pings from `extractOneProduct`, which the free path
calls. FREE-TIER-SPEC section 3 lists IndexNow as not free.

### 2.16 Product-limit grace never appears

`billing.server.ts:511` defines `limitState`; nothing calls it. BILLING-SPEC
section 3's banner and grace refusal do not exist.

### 2.17 The empty-string versus never-set question was never answered

`seo.server.ts:201` reverts by writing `prev`, which is `""` for a field
that started empty. SEO-WORKSPACE-PRD section 9 says to settle this before
the bulk queue ships. It shipped. Needs one dev-store write and read-back,
and the answer recorded next to `revertSeo`.

## 3. Improve

- No estimated duration on any long job (`app._index.tsx:466-474`,
  `app.collections.tsx:190-197`, `app.seo.tsx:1050-1058`). The JobRun row
  has start time and progress; products remaining times observed
  per-product time is one line.
- Crawler verdict is never a fraction. EXPERIENCE-PRD section 5 wants
  "7 of 8 crawlers received the page"; both screens make the merchant count
  tiles.
- Crawler tiles use tinted `Box` backgrounds as status (`app._index.tsx:542`).
  Built for Shopify reviewers flag this; use `Badge` tone.
- Install lands on an empty dashboard. `shopify.server.ts:26-41` only
  upserts the shop; EXPERIENCE-PRD section 4 wants the free dry run and
  crawler check queued in `afterAuth` so first paint has numbers.
- Collections renders up to 30 tall cards with no filter, most saying
  "Nothing varies enough here to compare" (`app.collections.tsx:231-282`).
- The mirror opens with YAML front matter; the "View" link should say it is
  a machine-readable file so the merchant is not surprised
  (`mirror.server.ts:71-89`).
- Free-tier "used" state is a bare text link repeated on 25 rows
  (`app.products._index.tsx:639-641`); one banner instead.
- The Google-Extended and Bingbot names are not in `KNOWN_BOTS`
  (`crawler-hits.server.ts:25-36`), so requests under those names are
  silently grouped as `other`. Bingbot, Storebot-Google, GoogleOther and
  Google-InspectionTool should be recognised; Google-Extended should be
  counted separately as a name that cannot legitimately make requests.
- No aggregate table for crawler hits beyond 30 days
  (CRAWLER-HITS-SPEC section 4 wants 13 months of aggregates).

## 7. What I would add to make it easier to use, more sellable and more convincing

Each checked against the data model. Ordered by how much it changes what a
merchant believes, not by effort.

### 7.1 Attribution: "pages that exist only because of this app, and who read them"

`CrawlerHit.path` and `handle` record which mirror page, llms.txt or
agents.md was requested. None of those addresses existed before install.
So the dashboard can say, truthfully: "GPTBot read 41 product pages that
did not exist in your shop three weeks ago." That is a causal sentence, not
a count, and no competitor can write it because they publish a static file
and log nothing. It is a `groupBy` on path over data already stored.
This is the one I would ship first.

### 7.2 Reporting at a glance

The mockup at `F:\AI Visibility SHOPIFY\mockup-dashboard-at-a-glance.html`.
Six of its ten panels read data the app already stores (`coverage()`,
`coverage().byAttr`, `CrawlerCheck`, `summarizeHits`, per-product fact
counts, the product's own metafields). One needs four strings added to
`KNOWN_BOTS`. Two need a snapshot row. One, sorting by sales, is refused.
The panel that changes minds is the before/after: one real product, its
paragraph on the left, seven rows of data on the right, with nothing
rewritten.

### 7.3 Every finding produces the text to send

The WordPress mockup has "What to send your host" on one finding. Make it
the rule. A merchant blocked by a firewall cannot fix it; their host can,
and they do not know what to write. For "47 products say nothing", a CSV
with handle and the missing question, ready for a copywriter. Templates,
not features.

### 7.4 Show where each value came from

Click "oak" in the specifications, the sentence it came from lights up in
the description. This turns "nothing is invented" from a promise into
something a doubting merchant verifies in two seconds. Caveat: `Fact` is
`{ k, v }` with no offset (`app/engine/extract.ts:10`), so the highlight is
reconstructed by searching the description at display time and can miss
when the engine trimmed a phrase. It must fall back to "found in your
description" rather than error.

### 7.5 A single "what this app has written" ledger with undo

One screen: every product touched, when, which fields, and a revert per
product. `writeFacts`'s withdrawal branch and `revertSeo` already exist;
this is a list over the `state` metafield plus two buttons. It removes the
largest install objection, "what if I do not like it", without ever
raising the exit clause: it is a product control, not a relationship term.

### 7.6 First paint with numbers

Queue the free dry run and the crawler check in `afterAuth`
(EXPERIENCE-PRD section 4). The merchant's first screen then shows their
own catalogue's readability and their own crawler verdict instead of
dashes. Nothing sells like the merchant's own data on the first screen.

### 7.7 The refusals where doubt happens

The three refusals live only on the Plan screen (`app.plans.tsx:322-360`),
which a subscribed merchant never opens again. Put "no orders, no
customers, nothing invented, nothing you wrote is touched" as one quiet line
on the product editor, where the merchant is looking at a real write and
deciding whether to trust it.

### 7.8 The Google AI Mode and Copilot sentence

Verified today on Google's and Bing's crawler documentation: Google crawls
for AI Overviews and AI Mode with the ordinary Googlebot user agent, and
Google-Extended has no user agent at all; Copilot reads the Bing index. So
no product can show an "AI Mode" traffic number from a log, and any that
does is modelling it. Say that on the crawler card. It is the same
argument as "no prompt sampling", and it lands harder because it is about a
number the merchant has probably been shown elsewhere.

### 7.9 What I would not add

Competitor comparison. It needs crawling third-party sites, opens accuracy
and legal questions in an app-store context, and moves the product from
"we measure your shop" to "we estimate the market", which is the ground
it is weakest on.

## 8. Suggested order

Wave 1, before Republica BIO is touched: 1.1 snapshot, 1.2 and 1.3 gates,
1.4 NaN, 2.1 editor feedback and revert resync, 2.5 password field, 2.11
term-gap noise.

Wave 2, the first sixty seconds: 1.5, 1.6, 1.7, 1.8, 2.3, 2.6, 2.10, 2.12,
plus 7.6 first paint.

Wave 3, what makes it sell: 7.1 attribution, 7.2 the at-a-glance screen,
7.3 send-this text, 7.4 provenance, 7.5 the ledger, 7.7, 7.8.

Everything in section 3 and the two tables can be folded into whichever
wave touches the file.
