## Why additionalProperty is absent, read to the bottom

The content block emits the facts fragment only when
`shop.metafields['$app'].theme_scan.value.ourProductNode` is true. Read live:

```
{"hasOrganizationLd":false,"organizationId":"","productId":"",
 "hasProductLd":false,"hasWebSiteLd":true,"ourProductNode":false}
updatedAt 2026-09-12T05:12:41Z
```

`ourProductNode` is false. It is written by `themeScanMirror` from **one**
product page - the first published product the Admin API returns - and that
product is:

```
products(first: 1, query: "published_status:published")
  -> card-cadou-republica-bio
```

which is one of the three pages in item 2 whose Product node is destroyed by
our own Liquid error. The scan read that page, correctly saw no Product node
of ours on it, and wrote `ourProductNode: false`.

**One bug, two symptoms.** The `comparison of String with 0 failed` error
found in item 2 does not only cost those three pages their Product node; by
landing on the one page the theme scan happens to read, it switched
`additionalProperty` off for all 189 products on the store. The fix committed
in item 2 fixes both, but only after `npx shopify app deploy` AND a re-run of
the theme scan on the SEO screen, in that order.

## The fragility underneath, reported and not worked around

The structured-data behaviour of a whole catalogue hangs on one arbitrarily
chosen product page. If that page is broken for any reason - ours, the
theme's, a draft, a gift card - every product silently loses
`additionalProperty`, and no screen says so. The app already holds better
evidence: the per-product page scan read 182 pages and found our Product node
on 179 of them.

Item 12 says to ship no workaround, so none is shipped and none is proposed
here as a patch. It is written down as a decision for Marius: whether
`ourProductNode` should come from the page scan's aggregate rather than from
one page.
