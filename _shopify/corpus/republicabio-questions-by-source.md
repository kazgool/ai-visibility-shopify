## What that means, said plainly

**565 of the 612 live questions on this store, 92.3%, are delivery, returns
and payment.** 46 are "who makes it". One is about a variant. Not one of the
612 is about what the product is, what is in it, who it suits or how to use
it.

The store's own descriptions answer all of those - 644 section questions and
291 merchant questions are sitting there - and both sources are off because
they are at 6.68% and 7.89% against a 1% bar.

So the honest summary of the buyer questions feature on Republica BIO today is
that it publishes the shop's shipping policy 189 times, correctly, and says
nothing about any product. That is a true statement and a working feature by
the bar it was held to; whether it is a useful one is Marius's call, and it is
the strongest argument in this batch for deciding what to do about the 1% bar.
