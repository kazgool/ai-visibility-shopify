## Common questions

**The dashboard says "queued" and nothing moves.**
Background work runs on our servers, not in your browser - closing the
tab loses nothing. If a job shows no progress for more than three
minutes, the dashboard will say so; write to us with your shop domain and
we will look at the job directly.

**Extraction found nothing on my products.**
The dictionary matches the language of your descriptions. Open
Dictionary, check the preset terms are translated into the language your
descriptions actually use, then run a preview: it writes nothing and
shows exactly what would be found.

**A value is wrong.**
Open the product in the app, correct it, save. Your correction is
permanent: the app records it as human-written and never touches that
field again. The automatic reading stays visible below for comparison,
and "Reset to automatic" hands the field back if you change your mind.

**The comparison table does not appear on my collection page.**
Three things in order: (1) Collections screen - does this collection show
"Comparison table", or does it say nothing varies enough to compare?
(2) Theme editor on a collection page - is the "AI Visibility comparison"
block added? (3) Is the app embed enabled? The dashboard's Setup card
verifies this against your live theme.

**The crawler check says assistants cannot read my store.**
The check tells you the cause it found - most often password protection
or a firewall rule. Diagnostics explains each cause and what to change.
Note that development stores always show the password page; that is a
Shopify restriction, not a fault.

**Does the app slow my storefront down?**
No. It ships no JavaScript to your theme; everything is static markup
rendered by Shopify. You can verify with any performance tool: there is
no request to our servers from your storefront pages.

**What happens to my data if I uninstall?**
It stays. Attributes, summaries and tables are Shopify metafields in your
store, usable by your theme without our app. Our copy of your settings
and job history is deleted within 48 hours of Shopify's redact webhook.

**Can I get a refund?**
Billing runs through Shopify. Write to us within 14 days of the charge
and we will refund it, no questions. After that, tell us what went wrong
and we will be reasonable.

## What we do not offer

No phone or live chat support, and no custom development. This keeps the
price at $99/year. Email reaches a person who knows the codebase.
