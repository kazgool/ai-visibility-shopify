## 4. Facts established that the next session should not rediscover

- `CrawlerHit.shopId` holds the shop DOMAIN, not `Shop.id` (comment in
  `app/routes/proxy.$.tsx`). Query with
  `WHERE "shopId" ILIKE '%republicabio%'`. My earlier "zero requests logged"
  claims were built on a join against `Shop.id` and were wrong: 66 rows for
  republicabio at 11 September 03:xx UTC, including a GPTBot test fetch
  (200), curl fetches of llms.txt, and browser visits. The counter works.
  No real AI crawler has come yet.
- Mirror URLs are `/apps/ai-visibility/<handle>` because Shopify gives an
  app no route outside the app proxy prefix. WordPress rewrites
  `^(.+?)/md/?$` because the plugin owns the server's routing; nothing on
  Shopify can intercept `/products/<handle>/md`. On uninstall the proxy
  route is removed and the mirrors, llms.txt and agents.md return 404.
  Metafields and SEO fields written into Shopify stay. "What was written
  stays written" is exact for data and does not cover served surfaces; the
  documentation should say so, and it is an answer to a question, never
  volunteered (CLAUDE.md rule).
- Shopify, 28 May 2026: `/agents.md` is canonical, `/llms.txt` mirrors it;
  a theme can customise root paths with `templates/llms.txt.liquid`,
  `templates/agents.md.liquid`, `templates/llms-full.txt.liquid`. This is
  the only way an AI crawler discovers the mirrors from the root; it is
  static Liquid, so it conflicts with "live, always current". Decision
  pending (section 6).
- The proxy mirror sends `Link: <product page>; rel="canonical"` and
  `<llms.txt>; rel="describedby"`, `Cache-Control: max-age=300`. Verified
  by Marius with curl; GPTBot user agent gets 200.
- ChatGPT product cards for the client's products currently cite resellers
  (biogama 151.93, Farmacia Tei 144.00 against the client's 149/155). That
  is retail-price competition, not a visibility defect on our side.
- Shella emits JSON-LD by hand. Files and lines to remove, for the client's
  developer: `sections/main-product.liquid` 87-130 (Product),
  `snippets/head-get-social-meta-tags.liquid` 54-61,
  `sections/header.liquid` 2085-2103 (Organization),
  `snippets/product-collection.liquid` 440-473. Keep `sections/header.liquid`
  2107-2119 (WebSite/SearchAction). The schema validator showed 3
  Organization and 2 Product nodes before the fixes; after
  deploy-2026-09-11-1 our Product node is held back on this theme until the
  developer removes theirs and B33 clears.
- The app embed had to be disabled, saved and re-enabled before the block
  appeared (`read-app-embed` showed 0 blocks, then 1). "Enabled by default"
  does not take effect on an existing theme; Marius asked that the default
  be dropped. Not done.
- graphile-worker 0.16.6, `maxJobExpiry` default 4 h not overridden,
  `worker/index.ts` handles no signals. A deploy during a job orphans its
  lock, the JobRun row stays "running", and the one-job-at-a-time guard
